Our Story

A love, in two acts

Sipho Junior and Boipelo reflect a truly special union between two wonderful individuals who amplify each other’s best qualities. There is unique harmony in their attributes; how they create the perfect emotional climate for each other; how they peacefully uplift one another or stand together as a united force. Beyond their complementary differences are beautiful similarities: the kindness, the empathy; the humble strength; the love for life and the love of laughter. Regardless of what is happening, there is always joy to be found in their presence. Their beautiful future together is a divine & precious gift to all our lives. We gracefully receive this, and offer them our total love.

"Two days, one forever." Sipho Junior & Boipelo
